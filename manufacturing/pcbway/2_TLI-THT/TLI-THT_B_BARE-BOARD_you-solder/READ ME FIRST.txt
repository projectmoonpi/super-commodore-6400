Assembly note: J2 (the VIC-II socket) is two 20-way socket strips on the TOP, one per row, soldered on the bottom, fitted AFTER J1 (its holes are one row in from J1's, so the two never share a hole).
Assembly note: Keep every lead on the bottom trimmed flush (under 1.5 mm): the board sits about 7 mm above the C64's motherboard.
